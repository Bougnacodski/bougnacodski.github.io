I'm Mathieu Besson. For about twelve years I have worked in data and machine learning architecture, in luxury and retail. Today I lead a team of data and machine learning engineers building data products, from business intelligence to generative AI.

I trained as a mathematical engineer and spent time in applied research. I still keep a foot in mathematics: optimisation, numerical methods, control.

## What you'll find here

- essays on organising data teams and on data governance;
- notes on applied mathematics, with equations and code;
- my side projects, whose code is open on GitHub.
